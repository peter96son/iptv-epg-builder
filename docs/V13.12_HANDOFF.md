## v13.12 first repair: CPS СССР / CPS USSR clock offset

Live observation in Los Angeles established that the programme sequence is correct but the clock is 14 hours early. “Женя, Женечка и «Катюша»” precedes “Мужики!..”; the guide showed the boundary near 07:11 while “Мужики!..” actually began near 21:11 LA.

Implementation:

- Do **not** change the CPS USSR EPG source.
- Do **not** shift other CPS/Openbox channels.
- `data/channel_time_offsets.csv` stores persistent per-source/per-source_id offsets.
- Exact active rule: `openbox-tsd + cps-ussr = +840 minutes`.
- `src/xmltv.py` applies the offset before freshness/usability checks and before yielding programmes, so `start` and `stop` move together and date rollover is automatic.
- `tests/test_channel_time_offsets.py` protects the +14h observation, next-day rollover, sibling isolation, and malformed CSV handling.
